## Purpose / user-visible outcome

After a proven automatic loss of an independent reviewer, a run with the implementer plus one known model-diverse reviewer can finish without a reviewer-count-only owner interruption. Non-count safety obligations remain binding.

## Known risks / de-risking

Manual/proactive exclusion without typed evidence remains attended. One reviewer has less redundancy; protect causality, different known model and independent evidence. Snapshot identity where native model observation is impossible is a disclosed limitation. Buffered output can remain silent until the hard ceiling; declaring it correctly prevents a worse false permanent dropout. Record the fresh goal-check duration; a healthy result over120s is evidence for a later timeout-tuning idea, not a scope expansion here.

Local fixtures establish lifecycle/classification behavior, not native provider latency or cross-host/Windows correctness. D6 accounting, Windows CLI limitations and held CLI WinGet, native-positive legacy quota recognition, alias/manual-edit guidance and persistent pre-idea proposal lifecycle stay deferred. Independent review must try to refute every AC; no disposition suppresses findings.
