## Summary

The 1.52.0 reviewer gate is cause-blind at exactly two code points, and that is the whole stall: `membership.CheckGates` hard-forces a minimum of 2 independent reviewers under `auto_implement` (`internal/membership/gates.go:82-87`), and the driver's LE-11 close gate compares a fixed `MinReviewers` (default 2 for deliberation, `internal/driver/driver.go:171-172`) against the current reviewer count (`internal/driver/impl.go:288-289`). Neither asks why the count fell, although the immutable cause already sits in the shipped dropout ledger. I propose a narrowly scoped **recorded-cause reviewer credit**: when every missing independent reviewer is covered by a recorded removal (a committed `participant-failure.v1` batch/kickoff candidate, or a §9.0 owner-confirmed `excluded:` record in the exact three-part shape), one model-diverse non-implementer reviewer satisfies the review round, review consensus, LE-11 close and the LE-7 goal check; with no recorded cause, nothing changes. Everything else the brief asks to "decide" is, on my reading of HEAD, already shipped and only needs the contract written down: watchdog verdicts already feed the two-attempt dropout path, the goal check already runs as a fresh process of the same non-implementer reviewer, mid-batch settlement already waits for all writers and preserves filed dissent, and the packet/SKILL budgets have 37 and 5 bytes of headroom, so the protocol hunk must be net-nonpositive in bytes.

## Proposed approach

### T1 — Recorded-cause reviewer credit (the new rule)

Amend `CheckGates` to take the recorded-removal set alongside survivors, via one shared helper (e.g. `membership.ReviewerMinimum`) also used by the LE-11 close gate, so the two gates cannot diverge. The credit: let `required` be the policy minimum (2 under auto). Compute `missing = required − len(reviewers)`. The gate passes iff `len(reviewers) ≥ 1` and **every** missing reviewer seat is covered by a recorded removal of an independent (non-implementer) participant, where a recorded removal is exactly one of:

1. a committed quota-history kickoff transition or batch candidate with `RuleID = participant-failure.v1` and valid supervisor-terminal evidence (`quota.History.Dropped` already derives this, `internal/quota/dropout.go:64-88`); or
2. a §9.0 owner-confirmed exclusion recorded in `00-prompt.md` `excluded:` in the exact shipped three-part shape `<id> — <reason> — confirmed <date>` (the shape this idea's own `00-prompt.md` line 6 uses for kimi-1). Any other prose, or a missing/malformed entry, earns no credit — the gate stays as today.

Counting both sources is forced, not optional: this idea has **no `quota-history/` directory** (verified: `ls` of the idea dir), so the dogfood case earns credit only through the recorded `excluded:` line. Credit never applies to a reduction with an unrecorded cause, never stacks with `standard`'s count-based degradation (that path is unchanged for non-auto ideas), and never lifts the `(auto||strict) && reviewers==0` goal-checker floor.

### T2 — LE-11 close gate and the other two CheckGates seams

The driver close gate (`impl.go:288`) computes its credit from the same helper over the current membership + history; `Complete`'s re-check (driver_impl.go:1002) and the consensus seam (consensus.go:378) inherit the fix automatically because they call `CheckGates`. This closes the exact gap the prior FINAL's D3/AC5 documented as blocking.

### T3 — Goal check: explicitly allow fresh-process same-reviewer

No mechanism change. FINAL should state the contract: under the credit, the LE-7 goal check **is** a fresh process invocation of the sole remaining model-diverse non-implementer reviewer, with its own durable step identity and preserved output; process freshness plus non-implementer identity is the independence floor, and the check can still only withhold a close, never establish one. I recommend raising the goal-check consult timeout from 2 minutes to a bounded configurable (default ~10 min) as part of this change: 2 minutes is tight for a criteria-sweep on a large tree, and a timeout here escalates uselessly. Small, optional, separable.

### T4/T5 — Diversity and strict gate read as-is

`require_model_diversity` already demands exactly "≥1 reviewer with a model different from the implementer's" — with one reviewer, that reviewer must differ or the gate blocks (gates.go:91-110). Unchanged. `strict_gate` likewise needs no new rule: the closing round is executed by the sole reviewer as a fresh full-scope Phase-6 round; independence comes from freshness + zero-findings certification + the veto-only scan (impl.go:237-265), and a strict idea still cannot close on NITs. FINAL should say both sentences explicitly so nobody re-derives them differently.

### T6 — Timing: write the contract, don't rebuild it

No new defaults, no new service. FINAL freezes as normative what HEAD already does: watchdog classes are dropout-eligible; first-output 120 s / stall 1,800 s clamped under the hard ceiling / heartbeat 60 s, per-agent spec override, `BuffersStdout` disables the soft guards by declared design (hard ceiling remains the backstop); the inner first-output retry consumes the second attempt slot; probes are deadline-bounded at 90 s per attempt. The one normative addition I propose: a dropout-eligible supervised step must not run with **both** soft guards disabled unless the agent spec declares buffering — i.e. config combinations that would silence a non-buffering agent for the full hard ceiling should fail closed at dispatch. That is a small pre-dispatch check, not a timing change.

### T7 — Retained dissent and mid-batch settlement: unchanged, restated

Settlement stays whole-batch after all writers stop; a failure landing mid-round/mid-consensus/mid-signoff-batch cannot apply a partial reduction, and the survivors' artifacts settle the round. A filed ❌/DISPUTED/finding by a participant who is later dropped stays binding (BLOCK returns early at consensus_request_signoffs.go:222-232; obligations captured at membership.go:331); only an owner ruling quoted into the next artifact or abandonment/v2 resolves it. Removing a dissenter must remain impossible — protected roles and the 2-usable floor are untouched by T1, which only relaxes the *reviewer-count* gate, never floor, protection, diversity, strictness or goal-check independence.

## Existing alternatives

Per §15.6(a), mechanisms this proposal builds by hand versus what the toolchain already ships:

- **ALT-1 — reuse `standard`'s count-based degradation (shipped: track.go:213-216).** Inherited mechanism, already in the tree. Reject as the vehicle: it is cause-blind — it would lower the bar for a 2-participant-by-design auto idea exactly as for a dropout case, and the brief conditions the change on a *recorded* dropout/exclusion. Constraint-forced choice, not preference.
- **ALT-2 — keep owner-attended close as the product path (shipped practice: this deck's last two ideas; pre-authorized again in the brief).** Constraint-forced today and it works, but it is the stall the owner wants gone: every dropout mid-idea waits for a human. Adopt only as the transition authority for this run (as the brief already does); reject as the shipped rule.
- **ALT-3 — blanket single-reviewer waiver under auto_implement.** Forbidden by the brief's non-goals ("blanket single-reviewer waiver") and unsafe: it removes the cause condition entirely. Reject.
- **ALT-4 — fix only the driver close gate (impl.go:288).** Smallest diff, but `CheckGates` (batch precommit, consensus seam, `Complete`) would still block first, so the run never reaches the fixed gate. Reject; the two gates must move together (hence the shared helper).
- **ALT-5 — new liveness/timing service for mid-round hang detection.** Nothing needs building: watchdogs already classify `no_first_output`/`stalled`/`timeout` as dropout-eligible evidence (dropout.go:30-39) with bounded detection latency. Reject (also barred: "no new retry service").
- **ALT-6 — raise the 70,000 B / 20,000 B budgets.** Explicitly out of scope (brief, and prior Z4). Reject; compaction instead.
- **Hand-built vs shipped within T1:** the removal *evidence*, ledger, history query (`History.Dropped`), linkage validation and batch machinery are all shipped and reused; the only new code is the credit arithmetic and the `excluded:`-shape parser. The `standard` degradation stays for non-auto ideas; the new credit is deliberately a separate seam rather than an overload of `PolicyFor`, because `PolicyFor` sees counts, not history.

## Concerns / open questions

1. **`excluded:` is softer than supervisor evidence.** A prose frontmatter line can be hand-edited; a quota-history candidate cannot. I require the exact three-part shape + confirmation date and nothing else, but should the credit additionally require corroborating source-context evidence (as kimi's file provides)? I lean no — the §9.0 exclusion itself already required owner confirmation — but this is the main integrity boundary of the design and deserves round-2 scrutiny.
2. **Kickoff vs mid-idea drops.** The owner's words cover dropout "for any reason", and §9.0 records kickoff exclusions too; I extend credit to both. Is there an argument to credit only mid-idea drops (kickoff-excluded ideas could instead have opened with fewer participants)? I think not — this idea is itself the counterexample — but it should be a conscious decision in FINAL.
3. **Goal-check timeout (T3):** raise to ~10 min configurable, or leave 2 min untouched to minimize scope? I propose raising it; separable if disputed.
4. **Divergence risk between the packet's §9.0 rendering and SKILL.md's summary** if their wordings drift; suggest FINAL freeze both byte-exact and add/keep tests asserting the gate sentence appears in both (the packet live-test already guards size, not wording).
5. **Windows:** inherited Windows failures in quota/dropout paths remain disclosed (prior released note); nothing here is expected to worsen them, but the fix-up budget should not be spent on them.

## Risks

- **Weaker review independence is real.** One reviewer, however model-diverse, reviews and goal-checks; correlated failure of implementer+reviewer is more likely than of three. Mitigations kept: model diversity hard gate, refutation-default discipline, fresh-process goal check that can only veto, strict gate for strict ideas, zero-findings close, retained obligations and the owner's standing pre-authorization remaining available. Residual risk is accepted by the owner's own direction ("one is enough").
- **Cause forgery / prose drift** (concern 1): malformed records must fail closed to today's blocking behavior; tests must cover garbage `excluded:` values.
- **Two-gate divergence** if implementers patch one gate; the shared helper plus tests at both seams is the control.
- **Compaction losing a rule**: the §9.0 rewrite must be byte-measured against both budgets with the existing packet live-test and skill budget test green before merge; drift guard over the three COOPERATION.md copies.
- **BuffersStdout blind spot**: a buffering transport still cannot be prompt-detected; only the hard ceiling bounds it. This is declared, not fixed here; the T6 pre-dispatch check prevents silently extending that blindness to non-buffering agents.
- **Dogfood bias**: this run closes under the owner's attended authority, not the new rule; the new rule is not proven by this run's close, only by its tests. The released note should keep saying so.
