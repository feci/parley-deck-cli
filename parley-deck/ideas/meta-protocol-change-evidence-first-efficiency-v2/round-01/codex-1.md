## Proposed approach

Freeze twelve tasks by their original IDs/hashes. Allocate each of four solo
identities three tasks and each of six unordered pairs two tasks. Keep full-four
on all twelve. Counterbalance three arm orders and drafter/critic assignment
with a fixed published seed or explicit table before treatment. Share a monotonic
900-second deadline across each whole task-arm. Failed members remain assigned;
partial output does not become a completed arm.

Budget design must first prove an executable maximum for every charged call.
Keep one campaign ledger with separate pilot, packet and grading suballocations,
retaining unsuccessful launches and retries. Never refund a reserve merely
because the price parser returned null. If a selected CLI cannot enforce the
reservation, refuse before that call and record the cell not-run. A subscription
or a provider-reported zero cannot be assumed to be a zero economic cost.

Use two genuinely grading-only configurations with no task-author/candidate-
author role. Fresh sessions alone do not settle an ambiguous author-disjointness
contract; the manifest must define identities and disclose shared-model bias.
Keep deterministic tests primary, the original rubric, and unresolved factual
disagreement explicit. Grading-only observers have no quorum signatures.

## Concerns and risks

USD 15 feasibility is UNVERIFIED: no enforced per-provider dollar maximum has
been demonstrated here. The 36 pilot cells, 28 packet/canary/control calls and
separate grading are fixed obligations, with multiple phase calls inside pilot
cells. Splitting 15 by a call count would be an arbitrary allocation, not proof
that it is usable. A budget stop is honest partial execution, not full delivery.

The full-four pilot changes the treatment being estimated and cannot be combined
with historical full-six results as the same arm. No treatment is known to have
started in the available preparation, but final freeze must inventory actual
campaign state before relying on that observation. History, global defaults,
source implementation and frozen packet experiment remain separate authorities.
