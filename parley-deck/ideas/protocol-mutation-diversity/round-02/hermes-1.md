## D5 — The owner asked for RANDOMNESS; all four of us answered with STRUCTURE. §15.6(b) applies.

Serious argument first, before dismissal.

The owner's framing (translated from Slovak): "a bit of randomness ... mutation ... a completely different angle ... unique solution." PRIMARY from `00-prompt.md`: the owner's request is genuinely open ("No, and here is the measurement" is fully acceptable). PRIMARY from ga-notes.md §5: "mutation is a learned proposal operator with semantic priors, not blind random perturbation"; "random mutations [are] inefficient in large solution spaces". These are not contradictory — they are a hierarchy. Randomness at the ASSIGNMENT layer (who does the divergence, which axis) is compatible with structure at the CONTENT layer (what the divergence must argue). The design proposed here respects that: PRNG-drawn axis + seed = random assignment; named axis list (invert assumption / variable constraint / scale / zero-code / absent-stakeholder) = structured content; roles: carrier = structured mechanism; behavioural-tuple metric = structured evaluation; null endorsement = structured failure mode. The randomness lives exactly where it is defensible: selection of who and which axis, not in the argument produced.

Why genuine stochasticity — unstructured, unrecorded, without semantic priors — loses: (1) §5 (PRIMARY): uniform random perturbation of a 220,115 B prompt space is close to worthless; (2) determinism constraint (§11 / protocol rules): unrecorded randomness makes artifacts un-auditable; (3) failure mode (§5 of my proposal): a randomly-generated adversarial argument costs a full participant round (minutes + bill) and degrades consensus by forcing refutation of a position nobody holds; at n=4 and ~2 generations (§6), one wasted individual is 25% of the population for 50% of the idea's lifetime; (4) reproducibility: the seed must be recorded (`consensus.md`); any mechanism that does not record it violates the protocol.

Can a defensible design exist in which genuine stochasticity outperforms structure? Only if: the stochasticity is at the selection layer (drawn axis / assigned participant), the content is still structured (named axes), the seed is recorded, null endorsement is mandatory, and the mechanism is deferred until Leg 2 separates premature from correct convergence. That design IS what is proposed here. Any version that drops the named-axis constraint, the seed recording, the null endorsement, or the benchmark gate — is the version the literature (§5) and the protocol (§15 determinism, floor-cost, auditability) reject. So the suspicious agreement (§15.6b applies) is real: the four participants independently converged on "structured, not random" — which may mean the literature's finding dominates all four priors. The mechanism proposed here is not the owner's literal "randomness" but its defensible translation: stochastic selection of a structured divergence operator, deferred and measured. If the owner insists on genuine unstructured randomness, this file states plainly: it cannot work under the protocol's determinism and auditability constraints, and it violates the literature (§5) this round was asked to read.
