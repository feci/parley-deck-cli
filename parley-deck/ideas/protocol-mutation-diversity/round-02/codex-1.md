## Current proposal

### D1 — Carrier and cost

**POSITION:** no mechanism lives in production yet. Run one sealed benchmark using the existing
`roles:` prompt semantics; do not change the core, runner, live rounds, `consensus.md`, or
`FINAL.md`.

For each frozen kickoff target, invoke the same selected participant/model/config in three arms:

1. **Control:** its ordinary advisory role.
2. **Structured reframe:** one scheduled transform—`boundary`, `mechanism`, `representation`, or
   `objective`—with an alternative emitted only if endorsable; otherwise null.
3. **Stochastic semantic donor:** a recorded seed selects, without replacement, one bounded
   mechanism/constraint/test tuple from a disjoint frozen idea. The participant asks whether that
   donor, its inverse, or its abstraction transfers; it emits only an endorsable alternative or
   null. Randomness selects semantic material, not tokens, temperature, or a position the agent
   must defend.

The first batch uses 12 target ideas: 36 generation calls. Two blind evaluators each adjudicate
the three outputs for each target in one call: 24 evaluation calls. Cost is therefore 60 deep
calls plus recorded wall time. Only a passing first batch is repeated unchanged on 12 held-out
targets, for a hard maximum of 120 calls. **RECALL — cost limitation:** exact provider tokens and
currency remain unverified without telemetry; the call ceiling is still auditable.

### D2 — The gap

**POSITION:** V1 establishes a genuine competing-position gap. It is correctly left open in
production because neither existence nor intuitive appeal proves that filling it improves the
ratified result. §15.6(a) should continue doing its different tooling-reuse job; §15.6(b) should
continue recording correlated agreement and disconfirmation. Neither should be misdescribed as a
steelman generator.

### D3 — Minimum falsifiable measurement

Freeze the 24 targets, arm prompts, tuple schema, quality rubric, evaluator identities, seed
derivation, and pass rule before generation. For each treatment versus its paired control:

- primary diversity outcome: proportion of targets with at least one additional valid behavioural
  tuple after semantic-equivalence collapse;
- quality vetoes: no lower median constraint compliance, feasibility, provenance discipline, or
  decision usefulness, and no increase in material unsupported claims or forced-only advocacy;
- replication: the same treatment must improve the paired diversity outcome in both 12-target
  batches and pass every quality veto in both; evaluator disagreement is unresolved, never an
  automatic pass.

Failure, ambiguity, or a quality veto closes the proposal as a measured null. Passing only opens a
new protocol-change idea; it does not authorize a core change.

### D4 — The 2026-08-29 removal

**POSITION:** removal was right, but the claim that current §15.6(a) replaced the same function was
wrong. The old clause was an unvalidated, recurring judgment duty susceptible to ceremonial
steelmans and facilitator avoidance. Its deletion deliberately accepted a gap in exchange for a
smaller, checkable production protocol. Restore nothing until a bounded test shows the missing
function changes decision-relevant coverage without lowering quality.

### D5 — Randomness on its strongest case

**SECONDARY — `reference/ga-notes.md` §5:** blind random mutation is inefficient in large semantic
spaces, while semantic priors make proposal operators useful. That rejects temperature/noise; it
does not entail that selection among semantic priors must be curated.

**RECALL — HYPOTHESIS UNVERIFIED:** a seeded random donor from the deck's own disjoint alternatives
can outperform a short axis list because it samples cross-domain mechanisms and curator blind
spots. It can also fail through irrelevance, which is why nulls and quality vetoes are mandatory.
This is the defensible stochastic design the owner asked us to consider. If it does not beat the
structured arm under the replicated gate, genuine stochasticity is rejected here with evidence
rather than by suspicious structural agreement.

For the QD alternatives: **POSITION:** use behavioural collapse and novelty only as benchmark
measurement; reject production local competition without an independent quality axis; reject a
MAP-Elites terminal archive because Parley ratifies one authoritative `FINAL.md`; and reject an
island reset until a predictive convergence trigger exists.
